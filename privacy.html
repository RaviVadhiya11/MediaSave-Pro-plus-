Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. About this website

MediaSave Pro is a static website that helps visitors download direct media-file URLs when the remote host permits browser access. It does not provide a server-side downloader.

2. Download history

Recent file names and timestamps may be stored in local storage in your own browser. This data is not sent to a MediaSave Pro server. Use Clear history or your browser site-data settings to remove it.

3. Third-party hosts

When you enter a URL, your browser connects directly to that host. The host may receive your IP address and process data under its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies and analytics

This starter site does not include advertising cookies or analytics trackers. Update this policy if you add third-party services later.

5. Contact

Use the Contact page for questions. Replace the sample email before publishing.
