Help center

Frequently asked questions

Why is my Instagram Reel URL rejected?

A Reel URL points to a social webpage, not a direct MP4 file. This tool intentionally does not extract platform streams.

Why can a direct URL still fail?

The host may require authentication, block cross-origin requests (CORS), or return a webpage instead of a file.

Where is history stored?

History is saved in this browser's local storage and is not uploaded to MediaSave Pro.

What does the backend do?

The optional API currently provides service health checks and a safe integration point for authorized provider APIs. It is not a social-media extractor.

Privacy policy

Your privacy matters.

Theme preference and recent file names/timestamps are stored locally in your browser. The website does not send this history to its backend. When you preview or download a URL, your browser contacts the URL's host, which may receive your IP address and apply its own privacy policy. This starter project includes no analytics trackers or advertising cookies. If you add third-party services, update this policy accordingly.
